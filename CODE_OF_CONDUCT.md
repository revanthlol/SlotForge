# Code of Conduct

SlotForge contributors are expected to keep collaboration respectful, specific, and constructive.

We welcome people of every background and experience level. Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, or sustained disruption are not acceptable in project spaces.

Assume good intent, critique ideas and code rather than people, explain technical objections with evidence, and make room for questions. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to [workofotb@gmail.com](mailto:workofotb@gmail.com). Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
